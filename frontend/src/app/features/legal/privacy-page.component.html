PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: July 31, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information the GameVN guild website and its companion Discord bot (the "Service") collect, how it is used, and your choices. This is a volunteer-run, non-commercial community project. We collect the minimum data needed to recognise guild members and show them the right pages — we do not sell or rent your data, and we do not run advertising or third-party trackers.

2. Information We Collect

When you log in with Discord, we request only the identify scope, which gives us:

  • your Discord user ID;
  • your Discord username and avatar.

We never request your email address, your other Discord servers, or your Discord password — Discord's own login page handles your password, and we never see it.

Beyond your Discord identity, we may hold:

  • your in-game name (IGN) and role, as recorded on the guild roster by guild leadership;
  • in-game statistics for guild members (level, gear, mastery, activity), synced from the game's own API/leaderboards;
  • match and footage records that guild members register (opponent, date, footage links);
  • basic technical logs kept by our hosting provider (e.g. request timestamps, IP address, error logs) for security and troubleshooting.

3. How We Use This Information

  • to sign you in and recognise you as a guild member;
  • to show you the roster, your rank/permissions, and (if you are a member) member-only pages;
  • to display guild-wide stats, schedules, match history and Hall of Fame placements;
  • to operate the Discord bot's guild-related commands;
  • to keep the Service secure and working (e.g. rate limiting, diagnosing errors).

4. Where Your Data Is Stored

Data is stored in a Microsoft Azure-hosted database and application, secured over HTTPS. After you log in, your browser holds a short-lived login token (and a small cached copy of your session) in its own local storage, so you don't have to log in again on every visit; this token expires automatically and is removed on logout. We do not use tracking cookies.

5. Who We Share Data With

We do not sell or rent your information. It is shared only with the service providers needed to run the Service — Discord (for authentication) and Microsoft Azure (hosting) — and only to the extent necessary for them to provide that infrastructure. We may disclose information if required to do so by law.

6. How Long We Keep It

Roster and stats data is kept for as long as you are associated with the guild roster, so the Service can show accurate, up-to-date information. If you leave the guild or ask us to remove your data, we will delete or anonymise it within a reasonable time, except where we need to keep limited records (e.g. security logs) for a legitimate purpose.

7. Your Choices & Rights

You can, at any time:

  • log out, which removes your login token from this device;
  • ask us what data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or delete your data.

To do any of this, contact us via our Discord server.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and Discord login itself requires meeting Discord's minimum age. We do not knowingly collect data from children below that age.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed. Material changes will be announced in our Discord server.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or requests about your data, can be raised in our Discord server. See also our Terms & Conditions.
